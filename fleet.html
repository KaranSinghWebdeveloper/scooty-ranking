Rental Requirements & Policies

We keep the vehicle renting process simple, honest, and fast so you can focus on your spiritual visit to Neem Karoli Baba Ashram without transport worries.

  • Original Documents Required: 1 valid government photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID) and an active Driving License for geared/non-geared two-wheelers.
  • Fuel Policy: Vehicles are delivered with sufficient fuel to reach the nearest fuel station in Bhowali (6 km away). Vehicles should be returned with the same fuel level.
  • Security Deposit: Nil or minimal refundable deposit depending on the chosen vehicle and rental duration.
  • Complimentary Extras: 2 ISI-marked certified helmets and complete digital vehicle documents.
