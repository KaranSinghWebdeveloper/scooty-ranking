Scooty Rental in Bhowali

Your gateway to Kainchi Dham, Nainital, and the Kumaon Lake District

Bhowali — The Crossroads of Kumaon

Bhowali is a small market town at 1,706 meters elevation, sitting at the junction of roads leading to Nainital, Almora, Mukteshwar, and Kainchi Dham. If you are arriving by train at Kathgodam Railway Station (28 km away) or by bus, Bhowali is likely the first significant town you pass through before heading towards Kainchi Dham or Nainital.

The town itself is known for its fruit market (especially peaches, plums, and apricots in summer), a historic TB Sanatorium built during the British era, and its position as a convenient hub for exploring the entire Kumaon Lake District.

How to Get a Scooty Near Bhowali

Our rental counter is located at Kainchi Dham, just 9 km (15–20 minutes) from Bhowali on the Almora highway. While we are based at Kainchi Dham rather than in Bhowali town itself, the short distance makes it easy for travelers passing through Bhowali to pick up a scooter on their way to the Ashram.

You can reach our pickup point at Main Market, opposite Gate No. 2 of Neem Karoli Baba Ashram. If you are arriving in Bhowali and heading to Kainchi Dham, simply take a shared auto or taxi for the short 9 km ride, then pick up your scooter for the rest of your trip.

Why Rent a Scooty Instead of a Taxi

A taxi from Bhowali to Kainchi Dham costs ₹400–600 one way. A round trip to Nainital via Kainchi Dham can easily cost ₹1,500–2,000 by taxi. In contrast, renting a scooty for an entire day starts at just ₹500, and you get the freedom to stop wherever you want, take detours to viewpoints, and avoid being stuck in car traffic on the narrow hill roads.

Bhowali also has the nearest petrol station to Kainchi Dham (6 km away), so fueling up is convenient and inexpensive for a scooty.

Best Day Trip Routes from Bhowali on a Scooty

Route 1: Bhowali → Kainchi Dham → Gagar Viewpoint

Start early in Bhowali, ride to Kainchi Dham for morning darshan at the Ashram (opens at 6:30 AM), then continue 14 km further to Gagar for stunning panoramic views of the Himalayan range including Nanda Devi. Return via the same route. Total distance: ~50 km round trip.

Route 2: Bhowali → Bhimtal → Sattal → Naukuchiatal

The "Lake Circuit" takes you through three of Kumaon's most beautiful lakes. Bhimtal is the largest with boating facilities and an island aquarium. Sattal is a peaceful cluster of seven lakes surrounded by oak and pine forests. Naukuchiatal (nine-corner lake) is the deepest lake in the region and great for kayaking. Total distance: ~45 km round trip.

Route 3: Bhowali → Nainital Mall Road

The classic Nainital trip. Ride 11 km uphill to the Mall Road along Naini Lake. Visit Snow View Point (accessible by ropeway), Tiffin Top, and the Naina Devi Temple. Be aware that two-wheelers can be parked near Tallital or Mallital ends, not on the main Mall Road itself (which is a walking zone). Total distance: ~22 km round trip.

Practical Tips for Riding Around Bhowali

  • Fuel up in Bhowali — there is a petrol pump right in the town center. Kainchi Dham does not have a fuel station.
  • ATMs and shops — Bhowali has SBI and PNB ATMs, medical stores, and small restaurants. Stock up on essentials here if heading to quieter destinations.
  • Weather check — Bhowali gets fog in winter (Dec–Feb) and heavy rain during monsoon (Jul–Sep). Check conditions before planning a scooty trip to higher elevations like Mukteshwar.
  • Road condition — the Bhowali–Kainchi Dham stretch is a well-maintained national highway but has many sharp curves. Ride cautiously, especially during rain.
