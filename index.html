Affordable Scooty & Scooter Rental Near Neem Karoli Baba Ashram

Kainchi Dham Scooty Rentals offers well-maintained two-wheelers for visitors traveling to the famous Neem Karoli Baba Ashram and the surrounding Kumaon hills. Our pickup point is located at Main Market, opposite Gate No. 2 of the Ashram, making it the most convenient rental spot in the area.

Choose from our fleet of Honda Activa 6G, TVS Ntorq 125, and eco-friendly electric scooters. Every rental includes two ISI-certified helmets, full vehicle documentation, and roadside assistance. Rates start at just ₹500 per day with minimal paperwork — bring your Driving License and an Aadhaar card, and you are ready to ride.

Why Rent a Scooty in Kainchi Dham?

  • The narrow Bhowali–Almora highway sees heavy car traffic during weekends and festivals. A scooty lets you bypass jams and park easily near the temple.
  • Explore nearby destinations like Nainital (18 km), Bhowali (9 km), Bhimtal, Sattal, and Mukteshwar at your own pace.
  • No expensive taxi fares — a full day of riding costs less than a single taxi trip to Nainital and back.
