Kainchi Dham Travel & Commute Guide

Everything you need to know before visiting Shri Neem Karoli Baba Ashram

About Kainchi Dham Ashram

Situated along the Nainital-Almora highway at an elevation of 1,400 meters, Kainchi Dham is a revered shrine dedicated to Lord Hanuman and the renowned saint Neem Karoli Baba (Maharaj-ji). Founded in the 1960s, it attracts devotees from across the globe seeking peace and spiritual solace.

Distances from Nearby Hubs

  • From Bhowali: 9 km (~15 to 20 minutes)
  • From Nainital: 18 km (~45 minutes)
  • From Kathgodam Railway Station: 37 km (~1.5 hours)
  • From Pantnagar Airport: 71 km (~2.5 hours)

Why Renting a Scooty is the Best Way to Travel

During weekends, summer holidays, and the annual 15th June Foundation Day Bhandara, traffic bottlenecks along the narrow Bhowali-Almora stretch can cause vehicle delays of 2 to 4 hours. Four-wheeler parking is extremely limited near the temple gate.

Hiring a scooter allows you to easily bypass car jams, park safely in designated two-wheeler spots, and tour neighboring viewpoints like Mukteshwar, Bhimtal, and Gagar without paying high taxi fares.

Ashram Daily Timings

  • Morning Opening: 6:30 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Evening Opening: 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM
  • Evening Aarti: Approximately 7:00 PM (Sunset timing)
